# DeepSupU-Net: Multi-Scale Attention-Guided U-Net with Deep Supervision for Breast Ultrasound Image Segmentation

Accurate tumor segmentation in breast ultrasound images is challenged by speckle noise, low contrast at lesion boundaries, irregular morphology, and severe class imbalance. This study proposes DeepSupU-Net, an improved U-Net architecture integrating four targeted enhancements: an EfficientNet-B5 encoder pre-trained on ImageNet-1K for richer hierarchical feature extraction; Convolutional Block Attention Modules (CBAM) at all four skip connections for sequential channel-wise and spatial recalibration of noisy encoder activations; a lightweight Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling module (ASPP-lite) at the bottleneck aggregating multi-scale context via parallel dilated convolutions at rates [1, 6, 12, 18]; and deep supervision through auxiliary segmentation heads at decoder stages 2 and 3 to stabilize gradient flow and accelerate convergence. Training employs a three-component loss combining Tversky, Focal, and morphological Boundary Loss (0.4:0.3:0.3) to address class imbalance, hard-example focus, and boundary precision simultaneously, optimized via AdamW with Cosine Annealing and Warm Restarts over 1,000 epochs. Evaluated on two public benchmarks, the model achieves a Dice coefficient of 87.73 ± 1.22% and a Jaccard index of 79.58 ± 2.01% on the BUSI dataset, outperforming strong competitors including ASFCP-Net, CAU-Net, and AAU-Net. On the independent Dataset B, it attains a Dice of 91.46 ± 1.48% and Jaccard of 82.34 ± 2.17%, demonstrating robust cross-dataset generalizability. With 31.4M parameters, DeepSupU-Net establishes a new state-of-the-art for breast ultrasound segmentation, offering a clinically favorable balance between accuracy, sensitivity, and computational efficiency.
